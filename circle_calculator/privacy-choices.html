Privacy Choices

Circle Calculator does not require an account. Calculation values and history are stored locally unless you choose to share an export.

Advertising

Circle Calculator 2.0 and later shows no ads and has no advertising consent options, because it contains no advertising SDK and does not track you. If you still use a version before 2.0, update the app to stop the advertising processing described in the Privacy Policy; until then, your device's tracking and advertising settings (iPhone/iPad: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: Google and device privacy settings) still apply to that older version.

Usage analytics and crash reports

Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics run only if you agree in the app. You can change that choice at any time in the app's privacy settings; turning it off stops new analytics and crash data from being sent. Calculator values, results, history and file contents are never sent. To ask for deletion of data already collected, use the contact on the support page.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel an auto-renewing subscription in your App Store or Google Play account. Use Restore Purchases in the app after reinstalling or changing devices.

Delete local app data

Clear history from the app. Uninstalling the app removes its local data subject to operating-system backup behavior. Generated files already saved or shared must be deleted from their destination separately.

Provider requests

Apple, Google, and RevenueCat process limited purchase information under their own privacy policies. Requests concerning their records, including advertising records from versions before 2.0, must be sent through their privacy request channels.
